What is Scoundrel? How to win a run

Scoundrel is a single-player dungeon-crawler played with a poker deck. In Scoundrel's Quest / Scoundrels Quest each round deals hands of 4 cards: wield diamond weapons, swallow heart heals, and bleed against spade and club enemies. Weapons only bite twice against weaker enemies, fleeing is rationed, and only empty hands let you descend. Survive all 10 rounds to win the run.
